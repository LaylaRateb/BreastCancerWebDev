Prevention & Early Detection

Checking for Breast Cancer

Symptoms:

  1. Lump or mass in the breast or underarm area
  2. Swelling of all or part of a breast (even if no distinct lump is felt)
  3. Skin irritation or dimpling
  4. Breast or nipple pain
  5. Nipple retraction (turning inward)
  6. Redness, scaliness, or thickening of the nipple or breast skin
  7. Nipple discharge (other than breast milk), especially if it's bloody

Prevention

Prevention:

  1. Exercise Regularly
  2. Limit Alcohol Intake
  3. Keep a Healthy Weight
  4. Breastfeed your babies
  5. Get enough sleep
  6. Do NOT smoke
  7. Do an annual mammogram
