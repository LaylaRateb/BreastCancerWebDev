Breast Cancer Explained

Breast Cancer Facts

  • Breast cancer is one of the most common cancers in women worldwide.
  • Early detection through screening can greatly increase the chances of successful treatment.
  • Breast cancer can affect both men and women, though it is much more common in women.
  • Genetics, lifestyle, and environmental factors can all influence the risk of developing breast cancer.
  • Regular self-exams and mammograms are recommended for early detection.
